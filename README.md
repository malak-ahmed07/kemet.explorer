1. Introduction
Kemet Explorer is a digital tourism application that helps travelers explore Egypt through a more convenient,
safe and personalised experience. It brings essential travel services into one platform: discovering
destinations, comparing transportation options, finding suitable food and exploring museums.
Recommendations and travel tools are based on each traveler's interests, preferences and needs, so users
can explore Egypt independently and in their own way. To understand whether the app and its digital
marketing activities are moving in the right direction, performance needs to be measured using clear and
practical indicators.
The KPIs below connect directly to the project's main goals: building awareness of the app, attracting and
activating users, delivering useful personalised recommendations, keeping travelers satisfied and
encouraging them to keep using and recommending Kemet Explorer.
2. Main Business Objectives
• Grow the user base: Reach 5,000 app downloads during the first six months after launch.
• Build active usage: Reach 1,500 monthly active users by month six and grow new users by 15–20%
each month.
• Deliver personalised value: Make sure users receive recommendations that match their interests, with
at least 60% of new users completing their preference profile.
• Keep travelers satisfied and safe: Maintain a user satisfaction rate of at least 90% and provide
accurate, trustworthy travel information.
• Build a recognisable brand: Increase awareness of Kemet Explorer among travelers visiting Egypt and
support future expansion to more destinations and services.
• Build user loyalty: Encourage users to return across trips and recommend the app to other travelers
